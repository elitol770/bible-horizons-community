# Governance and publishing

Project owner and initial repository administrator: **@elitol770**. The community proposes improvements; the owner controls acceptance, editorial direction and the official BibleHorizons service.

- Public contributors work in forks and submit pull requests. They receive no production or repository write permissions merely by contributing.
- `CODEOWNERS` names the owner for all paths. Required code-owner review and successful checks protect `main` for ordinary contributors. The owner retains GitHub's administrator bypass for emergency maintenance and owner-authored housekeeping; this is explicitly not a two-person approval guarantee for the owner. Routine contributor work should follow the review process.
- Bible, visual, rights and technical review are distinct. Record what was actually inspected and which revision; an AI review is advisory, not a fabricated human signoff. No reviewer is claimed to be appointed until they actually agree to that role.
- Repository acceptance means a useful community contribution, not official editorial approval or website publication. Rights, performance and full-chapter coverage can remain pending.
- There is no deployment workflow and no Cloudflare/payment/account credentials here. A trusted maintainer can import an approved, pinned package into the private application only after separately reviewing its contents. No automatic synchronization or execution of submitted scripts is permitted in the production environment.
- Maintainers may decline contributions, narrow scope or revert changes. Contributors may maintain their own licensed forks; only the owner controls BibleHorizons.com. Code/content licenses do not grant permission to imply official endorsement or use the project's brand as your own.

The initial review roles are a process, not a staffed service-level commitment. Public contributions and credit can persist under their licenses even if the official service later changes direction. Changing the project license does not erase existing grants or transfer other contributors' rights.
