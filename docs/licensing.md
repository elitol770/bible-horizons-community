# Licensing and commercial use

This repository deliberately separates public contribution materials from the privately operated BibleHorizons website.

| Material | Default terms |
| --- | --- |
| Kit source code, tests, schemas, scripts and configuration | [MIT](../LICENSE) |
| Original prose/docs, chapter proposals, scene data and artwork | [CC BY 4.0](../licenses/CC-BY-4.0.txt) |
| Third-party components or assets | Their recorded license; never automatically relicensed |
| Bible translations, notes, audio | Edition-specific rights; no blanket license from this repository |
| BibleHorizons name/logo, private app, domain, user data and service accounts | Not granted by these code/content licenses |

## What contributors are agreeing to

Original contributions use the applicable terms above. Creators retain copyright and may reuse their own work. BibleHorizons and other recipients may use and modify the licensed work commercially while complying with its conditions. MIT notices must remain; CC BY attribution must identify supplied creators, link the license, retain required notices and indicate changes. There is no exclusive grant or automatic copyright assignment. Do not describe the DCO as permission to relicense everything under any terms.

BibleHorizons may monetize its hosted service and use contributed material in paid offerings. Volunteer submissions do not create equity, employment, royalties or a compensation obligation. Any paid work or revenue share needs a separate explicit arrangement before work begins. Publicly licensed material remains reusable under its license; monetization does not revoke that permission.

## Initial asset intake policy

For the first intake, accept original material submitted under CC BY 4.0 and properly documented third-party **CC BY 4.0 or CC0** material. This intentionally narrow policy is not a claim that other licenses are illegal. Assets under other terms require separate review before submission/integration and cannot be passed through the current schema by renaming their license.

No NonCommercial, NoDerivatives, editorial-only, marketplace-restricted or undocumented assets in the initial intake. A download button, paid subscription or an AI generation credit is not proof of permission to redistribute source assets. Record source links, creator, license evidence and any modifications. Generated assets must name the tool, generation date and the applicable terms/evidence, as well as disclosing human edits. Such declarations still need a human rights review.

Do not upload copyrighted Bible text as a sample without edition-specific permission. The included example links to a publisher's passage and quotes no Scripture. Research links support the adjacent claim only; they do not license copying the source's images or prose.
